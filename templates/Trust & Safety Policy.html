Article 1 – Purpose of the Trust & Safety Policy

1.1 Objective

The purpose of this Trust & Safety Policy is to establish the principles, standards, procedures, and safeguards adopted by BeTogether to promote a secure, respectful, trustworthy, and legally compliant environment for all Users.

This Policy forms a fundamental component of the BeTogether governance framework and is designed to reduce risks associated with online and offline interactions facilitated through the Platform.

1.2 Relationship with Other Policies

This Policy shall be read together with:

  • Terms and Conditions
  • Community Guidelines
  • Privacy Policy
  • Cookie Policy
  • Disclaimer and Limitation of Liability
  • Content Moderation Policy
  • Applicable legal requirements

In the event of inconsistency, BeTogether reserves the right to interpret these documents in a manner that best protects Community safety and legal compliance.

1.3 Safety Philosophy

BeTogether believes that trust and safety are essential foundations of meaningful human connections.

The Platform is committed to fostering an environment where Users can interact confidently while respecting the rights, dignity, privacy, and security of others.

Article 2 – Scope of Application

2.1 Covered Individuals

This Policy applies to:

  • Registered Users
  • Visitors
  • Service providers
  • Service recipients
  • Event organizers
  • Event participants
  • Business accounts
  • Community members
  • Third parties interacting through the Platform

2.2 Covered Activities

This Policy applies to:

  • Profile creation
  • Messaging
  • Service listings
  • Service requests
  • Events and meetups
  • Reviews and ratings
  • User-generated content
  • Transactions
  • Offline interactions originating through the Platform

2.3 Geographic Scope

Because BeTogether operates internationally, this Policy applies globally regardless of the User's location.

Users remain responsible for complying with local laws applicable within their jurisdiction.

2.4 Continuing Application

The obligations contained within this Policy may continue to apply following account suspension, termination, or discontinuation of Platform use where necessary to protect safety or comply with legal obligations.

Article 3 – Guiding Principles

3.1 Safety First

Protection of users and the Community shall be prioritized whenever safety concerns arise.

3.2 Respect for Human Dignity

All Users are entitled to be treated with respect, fairness, and dignity.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and abuse are incompatible with the values of the Platform.

3.3 Transparency

BeTogether seeks to maintain transparent procedures regarding moderation, investigations, reporting systems, and enforcement actions.

3.4 Accountability

Users remain responsible for their actions, decisions, communications, and conduct.

3.5 Proportionality

Safety measures should be proportionate to the risks involved and applied fairly and consistently whenever possible.

3.6 Continuous Improvement

BeTogether may periodically update safety practices, technologies, reporting systems, and enforcement procedures to improve Community protection.

Article 5 – User Safety Standards

5.1 General Safety Expectations

Users must conduct themselves in a manner that promotes personal safety and Community wellbeing.

5.2 Responsible Participation

Users should exercise caution when:

  • Sharing personal information
  • Meeting others
  • Purchasing services
  • Providing services
  • Participating in events

5.3 Respect for Boundaries

Users must respect the physical, emotional, and personal boundaries of others.

5.4 Unsafe Conduct

Users may not engage in conduct that creates unreasonable risks to the safety of others.

5.5 Reporting Concerns

Users are encouraged to promptly report safety concerns through available reporting channels.

Article 7 – Events and Activities Safety

7.1 Event Participation

Participation in events occurs voluntarily and at the User's own risk.

7.2 Organizer Responsibilities

Event organizers remain responsible for:

  • Compliance with laws
  • Venue requirements
  • Safety planning
  • Participant management

7.3 Participant Responsibilities

Participants remain responsible for evaluating event suitability and personal safety.

7.4 High-Risk Activities

Activities involving physical exertion, travel, outdoor environments, or specialized equipment may involve elevated risks.

7.5 Safety Cooperation

Participants should cooperate with reasonable safety instructions issued by organizers.

Article 10 – User Verification Program

10.1 Purpose of Verification

BeTogether may implement verification programs intended to enhance trust, transparency, and accountability within the Community.

Verification measures are designed to reduce certain risks associated with anonymous interactions and identity uncertainty.

10.2 Voluntary and Mandatory Verification

Depending on the nature of the service, activity, or account type, verification may be:

  • Voluntary
  • Recommended
  • Required

for access to specific Platform features.

10.3 Verification Categories

Verification may include:

  • Email verification
  • Phone verification
  • Identity verification
  • Business verification
  • Professional credential verification
  • Address verification

10.4 Ongoing Monitoring

Verification status may be reviewed periodically and may be revoked where concerns regarding authenticity, compliance, or accuracy arise.

10.5 No Absolute Guarantee

Verification is a trust-enhancing measure and does not guarantee honesty, competence, reliability, legality, or future conduct.

Article 11 – Identity Verification

11.1 Identity Assessment

Identity verification may require Users to submit information or documentation sufficient to establish identity.

11.2 Acceptable Documentation

Examples may include:

  • Government-issued identification
  • Residence documentation
  • Passport
  • Driver's license
  • National identity cards

subject to applicable laws.

11.3 Authenticity Requirements

Users must provide accurate and authentic information.

Submission of:

  • Forged documents
  • Altered documents
  • Fraudulent credentials
  • Misleading information

is strictly prohibited.

11.4 Verification Reviews

BeTogether reserves the right to conduct additional reviews whenever reasonable concerns arise.

11.5 Verification Revocation

Verification status may be removed at any time where authenticity cannot be confirmed.

Article 12 – Business Verification

12.1 Business Trust Standards

Organizations using the Platform may be required to complete business verification procedures.

12.2 Verification Objectives

Business verification seeks to improve transparency concerning:

  • Legal business identity
  • Contact information
  • Registration status
  • Business legitimacy

12.3 Supporting Documentation

BeTogether may request documentation including:

  • Business registration certificates
  • Tax registration information
  • Regulatory licenses
  • Proof of ownership

12.4 Continuing Accuracy

Businesses must maintain current and accurate information.

12.5 Verification Limitations

Business verification does not constitute endorsement, certification, or approval of any business activity.

Article 13 – Verification Limitations

13.1 No Guarantee of Safety

Verification does not eliminate risks associated with human behavior.

13.2 No Guarantee of Competence

Verification does not confirm:

  • Professional skills
  • Service quality
  • Technical expertise
  • Reliability

13.3 No Guarantee of Future Conduct

A verified User may subsequently engage in misconduct.

13.4 Independent Evaluation Required

Users remain responsible for conducting independent assessments before entering relationships, transactions, or agreements.

13.5 User Acknowledgment

Users acknowledge that verification is only one factor among many relevant to risk assessment.

Article 14 – Trust Indicators and Badges

14.1 Purpose

BeTogether may display badges, indicators, or trust markers to provide additional information regarding completed verification procedures.

14.2 Informational Function

Trust indicators are informational tools only.

They should not be interpreted as guarantees.

14.3 Badge Removal

BeTogether reserves the right to remove badges where:

  • Verification expires
  • Compliance concerns arise
  • Information becomes inaccurate
  • Policy violations occur

14.4 Misuse Prohibition

Users may not falsely represent or misuse trust indicators.

14.5 Enforcement

Misrepresentation of verification status may result in suspension or account termination.

Article 15 – Reporting Violations

15.1 Community Participation

Community safety depends upon active reporting of misconduct.

15.2 Reportable Conduct

Users are encouraged to report:

  • Fraud
  • Harassment
  • Threats
  • Scams
  • Impersonation
  • Dangerous behavior
  • Policy violations

15.3 Reporting Channels

Reports may be submitted through designated Platform reporting mechanisms.

15.4 Good Faith Reporting

Reports should be submitted honestly and in good faith.

15.5 Confidential Handling

Reports may be reviewed confidentially to the extent reasonably possible.

Article 16 – Reporting Safety Incidents

16.1 Serious Incidents

Users should promptly report incidents involving:

  • Physical threats
  • Violence
  • Serious harassment
  • Fraudulent schemes
  • Dangerous conduct

16.2 Timely Reporting

Prompt reporting assists in protecting the Community.

16.3 Evidence Preservation

Users are encouraged to preserve relevant evidence whenever safe and lawful to do so.

16.4 Review Procedures

Reported incidents may be reviewed by Trust & Safety personnel.

16.5 Platform Action

Appropriate enforcement measures may be implemented following review.

Article 17 – Emergency Reporting

17.1 Immediate Threats

Where an immediate threat to life, health, or safety exists, Users should first contact local emergency services.

17.2 Platform Notification

After contacting emergency services, Users may notify BeTogether of the incident.

17.3 High-Risk Situations

Examples include:

  • Credible threats of violence
  • Serious criminal conduct
  • Child exploitation concerns
  • Human trafficking concerns
  • Imminent safety risks

17.4 Emergency Escalation

BeTogether may prioritize emergency reports for expedited review.

17.5 Cooperation with Authorities

Emergency situations may require cooperation with competent authorities in accordance with applicable laws.

Article 18 – False Reports

18.1 Accuracy Requirement

Users should submit reports that they reasonably believe to be accurate.

18.2 Prohibited Misuse

Users may not intentionally submit:

  • False accusations
  • Fabricated claims
  • Malicious reports
  • Retaliatory reports

18.3 Abuse of Reporting Systems

Abuse of reporting mechanisms undermines Community safety.

18.4 Enforcement Measures

Knowingly false reports may result in enforcement actions.

18.5 Protection of Legitimate Reporting

Users shall not be penalized for good-faith reports that later prove unsubstantiated.

Article 19 – Reporter Protection

19.1 Encouragement of Reporting

BeTogether encourages Users to report safety concerns without fear of retaliation.

19.2 Anti-Retaliation Principle

Retaliation against individuals who submit good-faith reports is prohibited.

19.3 Examples of Retaliation

Examples include:

  • Threats
  • Harassment
  • Intimidation
  • Review manipulation
  • Coordinated abuse

19.4 Additional Protections

BeTogether may implement measures intended to reduce risks to reporting Users.

19.5 Safety Priority

Protection of individuals who report legitimate safety concerns shall remain an important Trust & Safety objective.

Article 20 – Investigation Procedures

20.1 Purpose of Investigations

BeTogether may conduct investigations where reasonably necessary to:

  • Assess reported violations
  • Protect Community safety
  • Prevent fraud
  • Enforce Platform policies
  • Comply with legal obligations

20.2 Investigation Authority

BeTogether reserves the right to review information relevant to alleged violations, including:

  • Reports
  • Communications
  • Account activity
  • Verification records
  • Publicly available information

to the extent permitted by applicable law.

20.3 Neutral Assessment

Investigations shall be conducted in a fair, objective, and proportionate manner whenever reasonably possible.

20.4 No Obligation to Investigate Every Matter

BeTogether may prioritize investigations according to risk severity, available evidence, safety concerns, and operational resources.

20.5 Interim Measures

Pending investigation outcomes, BeTogether may impose temporary restrictions where necessary to protect Users or the Platform.

Article 21 – Evidence Collection

21.1 Information Sources

Investigations may rely upon:

  • User reports
  • Screenshots
  • Documents
  • Communications
  • Platform records
  • Verification information

21.2 User Cooperation

Users may be requested to provide information relevant to an investigation.

21.3 Authenticity Requirements

Users may not knowingly submit fabricated, altered, manipulated, or misleading evidence.

21.4 Preservation of Records

BeTogether may preserve relevant records where reasonably necessary for:

  • Safety investigations
  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Enforcement actions

21.5 Evaluation Standards

Evidence shall be evaluated based on relevance, credibility, reliability, and available context.

Article 22 – User Cooperation Requirements

22.1 Duty to Cooperate

Users agree to cooperate with legitimate Trust & Safety investigations.

22.2 Requested Information

Users may be asked to provide:

  • Clarifications
  • Documentation
  • Verification materials
  • Supporting evidence
  • Explanations regarding conduct

22.3 Timely Responses

Failure to respond within a reasonable timeframe may affect investigation outcomes.

22.4 Refusal to Cooperate

Refusal to cooperate may result in:

  • Account restrictions
  • Suspension
  • Removal of privileges
  • Enforcement actions

where appropriate.

22.5 Protection Against Abuse

Cooperation requests shall be limited to information reasonably relevant to the matter under review.

Article 23 – Confidentiality of Investigations

23.1 Confidential Review

Investigations may be conducted confidentially in order to:

  • Protect Users
  • Preserve evidence
  • Prevent retaliation
  • Maintain procedural integrity

23.2 Limited Disclosure

Information may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary to:

  • Conduct investigations
  • Protect safety
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Cooperate with authorities

23.3 Privacy Considerations

Investigation activities shall be conducted in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

23.4 No Public Disclosure Obligation

BeTogether is not obligated to publicly disclose details of investigations, enforcement actions, or internal safety procedures.

23.5 Protection of Reporters

Reasonable efforts may be made to protect the confidentiality of reporting individuals where appropriate.

Article 24 – Investigation Outcomes

24.1 Available Outcomes

Following an investigation, BeTogether may determine that:

  • No violation occurred
  • Insufficient evidence exists
  • A warning is appropriate
  • Restrictions are necessary
  • Suspension is justified
  • Termination is warranted

24.2 Risk-Based Decisions

Outcomes may consider:

  • Severity of conduct
  • Frequency of violations
  • Potential harm
  • User history
  • Community impact

24.3 Corrective Measures

BeTogether may require corrective actions before restoring certain privileges.

24.4 Finality

Certain Trust & Safety decisions may be considered final where necessary to protect Community safety.

24.5 Continued Monitoring

Accounts may remain subject to monitoring following investigations.

25.1 Moderation Objective

Content moderation seeks to maintain a safe, lawful, and respectful environment.

25.2 Review Mechanisms

Moderation may involve:

  • Automated systems

25.3 Scope of Review

  • Profiles

  • Images

25.4 No Universal Monitoring

BeTogether reserves reasonable discretion in determining whether content violates Platform policies.

27.2 Examples

  • Harassment

  • Hate speech
  • Dangerous misinformation
  • Encouragement of violence
  • Targeted abuse

27.3 Contextual Assessment

Moderation decisions may consider context, intent, severity, and potential impact.

27.4 Risk Mitigation

Where appropriate, BeTogether may implement measures short of removal.

27.5 User Safety Priority

Protection of Community safety shall remain a primary consideration.

28.1 Sensitive Categories

Certain categories of content may be subject to heightened review or restrictions.

28.2 Examples

Restricted categories may include:

  • Financial solicitations
  • Medical claims

Users may be required to satisfy additional verification or compliance requirements.

28.4 Platform Discretion

28.5 Regulatory Compliance

Restrictions may be implemented to satisfy legal or regulatory obligations.

Article 29 – Removal Procedures

29.1 Removal Authority

BeTogether may remove content that violates:

  • Applicable laws

29.2 Temporary Restrictions

29.3 Permanent Removal

Repeated or severe violations may result in permanent content removal.

29.4 Notification

29.5 Appeals

Article 30 – Warnings

30.1 Purpose of Warnings

Warnings are intended to educate Users regarding policy requirements and encourage voluntary compliance before more restrictive enforcement measures become necessary.

30.2 Circumstances for Warnings

Warnings may be issued for:

  • Minor violations
  • First-time violations
  • Unintentional misconduct
  • Correctable behavior
  • Low-risk policy breaches

30.3 Content of Warnings

Warnings may include:

  • Description of the violation
  • Relevant policy references
  • Required corrective actions
  • Potential consequences of future violations

30.4 No Obligation to Warn

BeTogether is not obligated to issue warnings before taking stronger enforcement measures.

30.5 Repeated Violations

Repeated violations following warnings may result in escalation.

Article 31 – Temporary Restrictions

31.1 Risk Mitigation

Temporary restrictions may be imposed where necessary to:

  • Protect Users
  • Prevent further violations
  • Preserve evidence
  • Facilitate investigations

31.2 Examples

Restrictions may include:

  • Messaging limitations
  • Listing restrictions
  • Content removal
  • Reduced functionality
  • Verification requirements

31.3 Duration

Restrictions may remain in place until concerns have been adequately addressed.

31.4 Compliance Requirements

Users may be required to complete specified corrective actions before restrictions are lifted.

31.5 Continued Monitoring

Accounts subject to restrictions may remain under enhanced review.

Article 32 – Account Suspension

32.2 Grounds for Suspension

Examples include:

  • Significant policy violations
  • Repeated misconduct

Suspensions may be temporary pending investigation outcomes.

  • Reverse decisions

34.5 Safety Priority

40.4 Security Measures

  • Loss
  • Misuse
  • Disclosure
  • Destruction
  • Alteration

40.5 User Rights

Eligible Users may exercise rights granted under applicable privacy laws, including rights relating to:

  • Access
  • Correction
  • Deletion
  • Restriction
  • Portability
  • Objection

subject to legal limitations.
