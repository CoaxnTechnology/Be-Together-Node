ARTICLE 1 – PARTIES

This BeTogether Ambassador Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

BeTogether, including its affiliates, subsidiaries, successors, assigns, administrators and authorized representatives (hereinafter referred to as the "Company", "Platform" or "BeTogether"), and the individual or legal entity approved by BeTogether to participate in the Ambassador Program (hereinafter referred to as the "Ambassador").

By accepting this Agreement electronically, the Ambassador confirms that they possess full legal capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement and perform all activities described herein.

ARTICLE 2 – DEFINITIONS

Ambassador

Means an individual or legal entity authorized by BeTogether to promote the Platform, register users, and support platform growth.

User

Means any individual or legal entity registered on the BeTogether Platform.

Direct User

Means a User personally registered by the Ambassador.

Exclusive Territory

Means a city, region or territory assigned by BeTogether to an Ambassador.

Direct Commission

Means compensation earned by the Ambassador from Direct Users.

Territorial Commission

Means compensation earned from Users active inside an assigned territory.

ARTICLE 3 – PURPOSE OF THE AGREEMENT

The purpose of this Agreement is to regulate the rights, obligations and compensation of Ambassadors participating in the BeTogether Ambassador Program.

  • Promote Platform growth
  • Increase user acquisition
  • Improve community engagement
  • Expand market presence
  • Support local communities

ARTICLE 4 – APPOINTMENT OF AMBASSADOR

  • Application through Work With Us
  • Invitation by BeTogether
  • Administrator nomination
  • Direct appointment by Company

Approval is entirely at the sole discretion of BeTogether.

ARTICLE 5 – INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

The Ambassador acts solely as an independent contractor.

  • Not an employee
  • Not a partner
  • Not a joint venture participant
  • Not a franchisee

Ambassadors cannot legally bind BeTogether.

ARTICLE 6 – ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

  • Minimum 18 years old
  • Legal capacity
  • Valid identification
  • Accurate information
  • Compliance with laws
  • Good reputation

ARTICLE 7 – USER REGISTRATION

Ambassadors may register new users only through official BeTogether registration procedures.

  • Provide accurate information
  • Collect data legally
  • Respect privacy laws
  • Follow platform rules

ARTICLE 8 – OTP VERIFICATION PROCESS

  1. User submits information
  2. OTP is generated
  3. User verifies OTP
  4. Account becomes active

Ambassadors must never:

  • Request OTP codes
  • Enter OTP codes themselves
  • Bypass verification
  • Manipulate registration process

ARTICLE 9 – USER MANAGEMENT RIGHTS

Ambassadors may assist users they personally registered.

  • Profile setup assistance
  • Onboarding guidance
  • Service listing support
  • General platform education

ARTICLE 10 – LIMITATIONS OF USER MANAGEMENT

The Ambassador shall not:

  • Access user accounts without permission
  • Request passwords
  • Request OTP codes
  • Access payment information
  • Perform financial transactions for users
  • Use user data for personal benefit

All management rights are limited to activities expressly authorized by BeTogether.

ARTICLE 11 – EXCLUSIVE TERRITORY ASSIGNMENT

BeTogether may assign an Exclusive Territory to an Ambassador at its sole discretion.

  • City
  • Region
  • Province
  • State
  • Country
  • Custom geographical area

Exclusive Territory assignments do not grant ownership rights and remain the property of BeTogether.

ARTICLE 12 – TERRITORIAL COMMISSION RIGHTS

Ambassadors assigned an Exclusive Territory may receive Territorial Commissions generated from qualifying activity within that territory.

  • User transactions
  • Service purchases
  • Platform activity
  • Eligible marketplace transactions

Commission eligibility shall be determined solely by BeTogether.

ARTICLE 13 – TERRITORY PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

To maintain an Exclusive Territory, an Ambassador may be required to meet performance standards.

  • Minimum user growth
  • Minimum activity levels
  • Community engagement goals
  • Platform promotion requirements

Failure to satisfy performance standards may result in territory reassignment.

ARTICLE 14 – TERRITORY REVOCATION

BeTogether may revoke an Exclusive Territory at any time.

  • Performance deficiencies
  • Violation of Agreement
  • Fraudulent activity
  • Platform misuse
  • Business restructuring

Territory revocation shall not create liability for BeTogether.

ARTICLE 15 – DIRECT COMMISSION STRUCTURE

Ambassadors may earn Direct Commissions from qualified transactions generated by Direct Users.

  • Commission rates are determined by BeTogether
  • Rates may change without notice
  • Special promotions may apply
  • Different products may have different rates

Commission percentages remain confidential.

ARTICLE 16 – COMMISSION CALCULATIONS

Commissions are calculated using BeTogether's official accounting system.

  • Completed transactions only
  • Verified payments only
  • Refund-adjusted amounts
  • Applicable deductions

The Company's calculations shall be final and binding.

ARTICLE 17 – BONUS PROGRAM

BeTogether may establish bonus programs to reward Ambassador performance.

  • User acquisition bonuses
  • Revenue bonuses
  • Territory bonuses
  • Performance incentives
  • Seasonal campaigns

Bonus programs may be modified or terminated at any time.

ARTICLE 18 – PAYMENT SCHEDULE

Commission payments shall be processed according to BeTogether's payment schedule.

  • Monthly payouts
  • Quarterly payouts
  • Special payment cycles

Payments may be delayed for verification, compliance, fraud review or operational reasons.

ARTICLE 19 – TAX RESPONSIBILITIES

Ambassadors are solely responsible for all tax obligations arising from commissions and bonuses.

  • Income taxes
  • Business taxes
  • VAT obligations
  • Local tax requirements
  • Government filings

BeTogether does not provide tax advice.

ARTICLE 20 – PAYMENT HOLDS AND INVESTIGATIONS

BeTogether may place temporary holds on commissions or bonuses where fraud, abuse or violations are suspected.

  • Fraud investigations
  • Duplicate accounts
  • Artificial activity
  • Policy violations
  • Legal compliance reviews

During investigations, payments may be suspended until review is completed.

ARTICLE 21 – CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

The Ambassador shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all Confidential Information received from BeTogether.

  • Business strategies
  • User information
  • Financial information
  • Marketing plans
  • Technical systems
  • Internal operations

Confidential Information may not be disclosed without prior written authorization from BeTogether.

ARTICLE 22 – NON-DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENTS

Ambassadors shall not copy, distribute, publish, sell, transfer, reproduce or disclose Confidential Information.

  • To competitors
  • To third parties
  • On social media
  • In public forums
  • For personal benefit

These obligations survive termination of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 23 – INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

All intellectual property associated with BeTogether remains the exclusive property of BeTogether.

  • Trademarks
  • Logos
  • Software
  • Databases
  • Designs
  • Marketing materials

No ownership rights are transferred to the Ambassador.

ARTICLE 24 – LICENSE TO USE COMPANY MATERIALS

BeTogether grants Ambassadors a limited, non-exclusive, revocable license to use approved promotional materials.

  • Marketing graphics
  • Logos
  • Brand assets
  • Promotional content

Such license may be revoked at any time by BeTogether.

ARTICLE 25 – DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY

Ambassadors must comply with all applicable privacy and data protection laws.

  • GDPR requirements
  • Local privacy laws
  • User consent requirements
  • Data security obligations

User data shall only be used for authorized Platform purposes.

ARTICLE 26 – COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS

Ambassadors shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations and governmental requirements.

  • Consumer protection laws
  • Advertising regulations
  • Privacy regulations
  • Tax laws
  • Business regulations

Failure to comply may result in immediate termination.

ARTICLE 27 – PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

Ambassadors shall not engage in prohibited activities, including but not limited to:

  • Fraud
  • Money laundering
  • False advertising
  • Spam marketing
  • Identity theft
  • Platform manipulation
  • Unauthorized account creation

Violations may result in immediate removal from the Program.

ARTICLE 28 – SUSPENSION RIGHTS

BeTogether reserves the right to suspend an Ambassador at any time where violations or risks are suspected.

  • Fraud investigations
  • Security concerns
  • Policy violations
  • Legal compliance reviews
  • Operational requirements

Suspension may occur without prior notice.

ARTICLE 29 – TERMINATION OF AGREEMENT

This Agreement may be terminated by BeTogether at any time.

  • Breach of Agreement
  • Fraudulent conduct
  • Misrepresentation
  • Illegal activity
  • Business restructuring
  • Program discontinuation

Termination shall immediately end all Ambassador rights.

ARTICLE 30 – EFFECT OF TERMINATION

Upon termination, the Ambassador shall immediately cease:

  • Representing BeTogether
  • Using Company branding
  • Accessing confidential information
  • Using Ambassador privileges
  • Claiming territorial rights

BeTogether may revoke all access permissions and require return or destruction of confidential materials.

ARTICLE 31 – LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

To the maximum extent permitted by law, BeTogether shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages arising from participation in the Ambassador Program.

  • Loss of profits
  • Loss of revenue
  • Loss of business opportunities
  • Loss of data
  • Reputational damage
  • Business interruption

Participation in the Program is undertaken entirely at the Ambassador's own risk.

ARTICLE 32 – INDEMNIFICATION

The Ambassador agrees to defend, indemnify and hold harmless BeTogether, its affiliates, directors, officers, employees and representatives from any claims, damages, liabilities, losses, costs and expenses arising from:

  • Violation of this Agreement
  • Violation of applicable laws
  • Fraudulent conduct
  • Negligence or misconduct
  • Unauthorized activities

ARTICLE 33 – NO GUARANTEE OF EARNINGS

BeTogether does not guarantee any minimum earnings, commissions, bonuses, profits or financial benefits.

Earnings depend upon numerous factors including Ambassador performance, market conditions, user activity and Platform growth.

Past results do not guarantee future results.

ARTICLE 34 – DISPUTE RESOLUTION

In the event of any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the parties shall first attempt to resolve the matter through good-faith negotiations.

If a dispute cannot be resolved amicably, the matter shall proceed according to the procedures established by BeTogether.

ARTICLE 35 – ARBITRATION

Any dispute that cannot be resolved through negotiation may be submitted to binding arbitration where permitted by applicable law.

  • Arbitration shall be confidential
  • Decisions shall be binding
  • Parties waive jury trial rights where permitted
  • Costs may be allocated by the arbitrator

ARTICLE 36 – GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed and interpreted in accordance with the laws designated by BeTogether, without regard to conflict of law principles.

Ambassadors agree to submit to the jurisdiction selected by BeTogether for matters not subject to arbitration.

ARTICLE 37 – FORCE MAJEURE

BeTogether shall not be liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

  • Natural disasters
  • Government actions
  • War or terrorism
  • Internet outages
  • Power failures
  • Cybersecurity incidents
  • Pandemics or epidemics

ARTICLE 38 – AMENDMENTS

BeTogether reserves the right to amend, modify or update this Agreement at any time.

Updated versions may be published on the Platform and shall become effective upon publication unless otherwise specified.

Continued participation constitutes acceptance of any modifications.

ARTICLE 39 – SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

Any invalid provision shall be replaced with a valid provision that most closely reflects the original intent.

ARTICLE 40 – FINAL PROVISIONS AND ACCEPTANCE

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between BeTogether and the Ambassador regarding participation in the Ambassador Program.

By accepting this Agreement, the Ambassador acknowledges and agrees that:

  • They have read and understood all provisions.
  • They agree to comply with all obligations.
  • They understand the commission structure.
  • They acknowledge BeTogether's ownership rights.
  • They understand territory assignments may be modified or revoked.
  • They accept all policies incorporated into this Agreement.

Electronic acceptance of this Agreement shall have the same legal force and effect as a handwritten signature.
