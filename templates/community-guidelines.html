Article 1 – Introduction and Purpose

1.1 Purpose of the Platform

BeTogether is a global digital platform designed to facilitate meaningful human connections through services, activities, experiences, events, networking opportunities, and community engagement.

The Platform aims to empower individuals and communities by providing a secure environment where Users can connect, collaborate, share skills, offer services, request assistance, participate in activities, and create positive social interactions.

These Community Guidelines establish the behavioral standards, safety expectations, and community principles governing participation within the BeTogether Platform.

1.2 Objectives of These Guidelines

These Community Guidelines are intended to:

  • Promote a safe and respectful environment.
  • Protect Users from harmful conduct.
  • Foster trust and accountability.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and illegal activity.
  • Support compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
  • Define acceptable and unacceptable conduct.
  • Establish transparent enforcement procedures.

These Community Guidelines complement and should be read together with the following legal documents:

  • Terms and Conditions
  • Privacy Policy
  • Cookie Policy
  • Disclaimer and Limitation of Liability
  • Legal Information
  • Trust & Safety Policies

1.3 Commitment to Community Safety

BeTogether is committed to maintaining a trustworthy Platform through proactive moderation, user reporting systems, safety tools, educational resources, and enforcement mechanisms.

However, Users remain responsible for exercising reasonable judgment when interacting with others through the Platform.

No online platform can eliminate all risks associated with human interactions.

1.4 Global Nature of the Platform

BeTogether operates internationally and may be accessed from multiple jurisdictions around the world.

Users agree to comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws governing their use of the Platform.

Where local laws impose stricter requirements than these Community Guidelines, Users must comply with the stricter legal standard.

Article 2 – Definitions

For purposes of these Community Guidelines, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below.

2.1 "Platform"

"Platform" refers to the BeTogether website, mobile applications, software systems, communication tools, APIs, services, and all associated digital environments operated under the BeTogether brand.

2.2 "User"

"User" means any individual who accesses, browses, registers for, interacts with, or otherwise utilizes the Platform.

2.3 "Account"

"Account" means a registered profile created by a User to access Platform functionalities.

2.4 "Content"

"Content" includes all information uploaded, shared, published, transmitted, displayed, or otherwise made available through the Platform, including:

  • Text
  • Images
  • Videos
  • Audio
  • Reviews
  • Messages
  • Listings
  • Event descriptions
  • Service offers

2.5 "Service"

"Service" means any activity, assistance, expertise, task, consultation, event, experience, or offering made available by one User to another through the Platform.

2.6 "Community"

"Community" refers collectively to all Users, contributors, moderators, administrators, partners, and participants utilizing the Platform.

2.7 "Verification"

"Verification" means any process used by BeTogether to assess or confirm specific information provided by a User, including identity, email address, telephone number, business credentials, or other relevant information.

Verification does not constitute a guarantee of trustworthiness, competence, honesty, or future conduct.

Article 3 – Scope of Application

3.1 Binding Nature

These Community Guidelines apply to all Users without exception.

By accessing or using the Platform, Users agree to comply with these Community Guidelines.

3.2 Coverage of Activities

These Community Guidelines apply to:

  • Profile creation.
  • Messaging.
  • Listings.
  • Service offerings.
  • Service requests.
  • Reviews.
  • Events.
  • Community interactions.
  • Content publication.
  • Offline interactions initiated through the Platform.

3.3 Off-Platform Conduct

BeTogether may consider conduct occurring outside the Platform where such conduct:

  • Originated through Platform interactions.
  • Threatens the safety of Users.
  • Damages community trust.
  • Violates applicable laws.

3.4 Additional Policies

Certain Platform features may be subject to additional policies, requirements, or rules.

Where applicable, Users agree to comply with such supplementary policies.

Article 4 – Acceptance of the Guidelines

4.1 Agreement

By creating an Account, accessing the Platform, or using any Platform feature, Users acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to these Community Guidelines.

4.2 Ongoing Compliance

Acceptance of these Community Guidelines is an ongoing condition of Platform access.

Users must continue to comply with these Community Guidelines throughout the duration of their use of the Platform.

4.3 Changes and Updates

BeTogether reserves the right to modify these Community Guidelines periodically.

Material changes may be communicated through:

  • Website notices.
  • In-app notifications.
  • Email communications.
  • Updates to legal documentation.

Continued use of the Platform following publication of revised Community Guidelines constitutes acceptance of the updated version.

4.4 Refusal of Acceptance

Users who do not agree with these Community Guidelines must immediately discontinue use of the Platform and cease accessing any BeTogether services.

Article 5 – Eligibility Requirements

5.1 Minimum Age Requirement

BeTogether is exclusively intended for individuals who are at least eighteen (18) years of age.

By creating an Account, Users represent and warrant that:

  • They are at least eighteen (18) years old.
  • They possess the legal capacity to enter into binding agreements.
  • They are legally permitted to use the Platform under applicable laws.

Any Account determined to belong to a person under the age of eighteen (18) may be suspended or permanently removed without prior notice.

5.2 Legal Capacity

Users must possess the legal authority to enter into contracts and assume legal responsibilities associated with services, events, transactions, and interactions conducted through the Platform.

Individuals who lack legal capacity under applicable laws are prohibited from using the Platform.

5.3 Compliance with Local Laws

Users are solely responsible for ensuring that their use of the Platform complies with all laws, regulations, licensing requirements, professional obligations, and restrictions applicable in their jurisdiction.

BeTogether does not guarantee that any service, activity, or opportunity offered on the Platform is lawful in every jurisdiction.

5.4 Restricted Jurisdictions

BeTogether reserves the right to restrict or prohibit access to the Platform in specific countries, territories, or regions where legal, regulatory, security, operational, or compliance concerns exist.

Users may not circumvent geographic restrictions through the use of VPNs, proxy services, or similar technologies.

5.5 Professional and Licensed Activities

Users offering regulated professional services must ensure that they possess all required:

  • Licenses
  • Certifications
  • Authorizations
  • Registrations
  • Insurance coverage

as required by applicable law.

Examples may include:

  • Legal services
  • Medical services
  • Financial consulting
  • Real estate activities
  • Transportation services
  • Educational services

The responsibility for regulatory compliance remains exclusively with the User.

Article 6 – Account Registration

6.1 Accurate Information

Users must provide accurate, complete, and up-to-date information when creating and maintaining their BeTogether Account.

Users shall not provide false, misleading, fraudulent, or deceptive information during the registration process.

6.2 One Account Per User

Unless expressly authorized by BeTogether, each User may maintain only one personal Account.

Creating multiple Accounts to evade enforcement actions, manipulate Platform features, or engage in deceptive practices is strictly prohibited.

6.3 Account Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their Account credentials, including passwords and authentication methods.

Users must promptly notify BeTogether if they become aware of any unauthorized access, suspected compromise, or security breach involving their Account.

6.4 Prohibited Registration Practices

Users shall not:

  • Create Accounts using false identities.
  • Impersonate another individual or organization.
  • Use stolen or unauthorized contact information.
  • Register Accounts on behalf of another person without authorization.
  • Create Accounts for fraudulent or unlawful purposes.

6.5 Account Responsibility

Users remain solely responsible for all activities conducted through their Accounts, regardless of whether such activities were authorized by the User.

BeTogether reserves the right to suspend or terminate Accounts that violate these Community Guidelines, applicable laws, or other Platform policies.

Article 7 – Identity Verification

7.1 Verification Process

BeTogether may require Users to complete identity verification in order to access certain Platform features, participate in specific activities, or enhance the security and integrity of the community.

Verification may include confirmation of a User's identity, contact information, government-issued identification, business credentials, or other information reasonably requested by BeTogether.

7.2 Cooperation with Verification Requests

Users agree to provide accurate, complete, and authentic information during any verification process.

Failure to cooperate with verification requests, or the submission of false or misleading information, may result in restricted access, suspension, or termination of the User's Account.

7.3 No Guarantee of User Conduct

Identity verification is intended to improve trust and safety within the Platform but does not constitute a guarantee of a User's identity, honesty, reliability, qualifications, future conduct, or suitability for any interaction.

Users remain responsible for exercising appropriate judgment and taking reasonable precautions when engaging with others through the Platform.

7.4 Ongoing Verification

BeTogether reserves the right to request additional or renewed verification at any time where necessary for security, regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, or the protection of the Platform and its Users.

Users who fail to satisfy ongoing verification requirements may lose access to certain features or have their Accounts restricted, suspended, or terminated.

Article 8 – User Responsibilities

8.1 Compliance with Laws and Policies

Users are responsible for ensuring that their activities on the BeTogether Platform comply with these Community Guidelines, the Terms and Conditions, applicable laws, regulations, and any additional policies published by BeTogether.

8.2 Respectful Conduct

Users shall interact with others in a respectful, honest, and professional manner.

Users must not engage in behavior that is abusive, intimidating, harassing, discriminatory, threatening, or otherwise harmful to other members of the community.

8.3 Accurate Information

Users are responsible for maintaining accurate and current information within their Accounts, including contact details, profile information, qualifications, and any other information relevant to their use of the Platform.

8.4 Account Security

Users must take reasonable measures to protect the security of their Accounts and shall not share login credentials or permit unauthorized individuals to access their Accounts.

Users are responsible for all activities conducted through their Accounts unless otherwise required by applicable law.

8.5 Reporting Concerns

Users are encouraged to promptly report suspected violations of these Community Guidelines, fraudulent activity, safety concerns, or unlawful conduct through the reporting mechanisms provided by BeTogether.

8.6 Responsibility for Interactions

Users remain solely responsible for their decisions, communications, transactions, and interactions with other Users, whether those interactions occur online or offline following contact through the Platform.

Users should exercise appropriate caution and reasonable judgment when participating in meetings, events, services, or other activities arranged through BeTogether.

Article 9 – Respectful Conduct

9.1 Fundamental Principle

Respect is the foundation of the BeTogether Community.

Every User is expected to treat others with dignity, courtesy, honesty, and professionalism regardless of their background, beliefs, identity, opinions, or circumstances.

9.2 Expected Standards of Conduct

Users shall conduct themselves in a manner that promotes a safe, welcoming, and trustworthy environment.

Users are expected to:

  • Communicate respectfully.
  • Act honestly and in good faith.
  • Respect personal boundaries.
  • Honor commitments and agreements.
  • Resolve disagreements constructively.
  • Support a positive Community experience.

9.3 Prohibited Conduct

Users shall not engage in conduct that:

  • Intimidates or humiliates others.
  • Disrupts Community participation.
  • Creates a hostile or unsafe environment.
  • Encourages abusive or harmful behavior.
  • Undermines trust within the Community.

9.4 Respect for Diversity

BeTogether welcomes Users from diverse cultures, backgrounds, and perspectives.

Users are expected to respect cultural differences, personal beliefs, and individual identities while interacting through the Platform.

9.5 Responsibility for Conduct

Users remain solely responsible for their communications, behavior, and interactions conducted through or arising from the Platform.

Conduct inconsistent with these Community Guidelines may result in warnings, restrictions, suspension, or permanent termination of the User's Account.

Article 10 – Anti-Harassment Policy

10.1 Zero-Tolerance Principle

BeTogether maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding harassment.

Harassment undermines Community trust and creates an unsafe environment.

Any form of harassment may result in immediate enforcement action.

10.2 Definition of Harassment

Harassment includes unwanted behavior that intimidates, humiliates, threatens, degrades, or disturbs another User.

Harassment may occur through:

  • Messages
  • Comments
  • Reviews
  • Images
  • Videos
  • Voice communications
  • Offline interactions

10.3 Prohibited Harassing Conduct

Examples include:

  • Repeated unwanted contact.
  • Persistent unwanted messages.
  • Sexual harassment.
  • Intimidation.
  • Threatening language.
  • Public humiliation.
  • Personal attacks.
  • Deliberate embarrassment.
  • Coercive behavior.

This list is illustrative and not exhaustive.

10.4 Sexual Harassment

The following conduct is strictly prohibited:

  • Unwanted sexual advances.
  • Sexual propositions.
  • Requests for sexual favors.
  • Explicit sexual content directed toward another User.
  • Unwanted sexually suggestive messages.
  • Sexual coercion.

Consent must always be respected.

10.5 Stalking and Obsessive Conduct

Users may not engage in behavior that causes another person to feel monitored, followed, intimidated, or unsafe.

Examples include:

  • Excessive unwanted contact.
  • Tracking another User's activities.
  • Following Users across multiple platforms.
  • Repeatedly creating new accounts after being blocked.

10.6 Harassment Outside the Platform

BeTogether may investigate harassment occurring outside the Platform when:

  • The relationship originated through the Platform.
  • The conduct affects Community safety.
  • The conduct creates risks for other Users.

Article 11 – Anti-Discrimination Policy

11.1 Equal Treatment Principle

BeTogether is committed to maintaining an inclusive environment where all Users are treated fairly and equally.

Discrimination is prohibited regardless of:

  • Race
  • Ethnicity
  • Nationality
  • Citizenship
  • Religion
  • Gender
  • Gender identity
  • Sexual orientation
  • Age
  • Disability
  • Marital status
  • Veteran status
  • Socioeconomic background
  • Any other protected characteristic.

11.2 Prohibited Discriminatory Conduct

Users may not:

  • Exclude individuals based on protected characteristics.
  • Publish discriminatory statements.
  • Promote discriminatory practices.
  • Harass individuals because of protected characteristics.
  • Encourage discriminatory conduct.

11.3 Inclusive Participation

Users are encouraged to foster an environment that welcomes diversity and respects differences in background, culture, beliefs, and perspectives.

11.4 Service-Related Discrimination

Users offering services through the Platform may not refuse services solely on the basis of protected characteristics where such refusal would violate applicable laws.

11.5 Accessibility and Respect

Users should make reasonable efforts to communicate respectfully with individuals who may have disabilities or accessibility needs.

Article 12 – Hate Speech and Extremism

12.1 Prohibition of Hate Speech

Hate speech is strictly prohibited.

Content or conduct that attacks, dehumanizes, threatens, or promotes hostility against individuals or groups based on protected characteristics is not permitted.

12.2 Examples of Hate Speech

Examples include:

  • Racial slurs.
  • Ethnic insults.
  • Religious hatred.
  • Calls for exclusion or segregation.
  • Dehumanizing language.
  • Advocacy of discrimination.

This list is non-exhaustive.

12.3 Extremist Organizations

Users may not:

  • Promote extremist groups.
  • Recruit for extremist organizations.
  • Disseminate extremist propaganda.
  • Encourage extremist ideologies.
  • Glorify extremist violence.

12.4 Terrorism and Violent Extremism

Any support for terrorism, terrorist organizations, or violent extremist movements is strictly prohibited.

Violations may be reported to competent authorities where legally required.

12.5 Educational and Historical Context

Discussion of historical, academic, journalistic, or educational topics involving extremist subjects may be permitted when presented in a legitimate informational context.

BeTogether reserves sole discretion in determining context.

Article 13 – Violence, Threats, and Dangerous Conduct

13.1 Commitment to Safety

BeTogether prioritizes the physical and psychological safety of all Users.

Conduct that threatens safety will be treated as a serious violation.

13.2 Threats of Violence

Users may not:

  • Threaten physical harm.
  • Encourage violence.
  • Suggest violent acts.
  • Use intimidating language intended to create fear.
  • Promote acts of retaliation.

Threats may result in immediate account suspension.

13.3 Incitement of Violence

Users may not encourage, organize, coordinate, facilitate, or celebrate acts of violence against any individual, group, organization, or community.

13.4 Dangerous Activities

Users may not use the Platform to:

  • Organize unlawful violent activities.
  • Facilitate criminal conduct.
  • Promote dangerous challenges.
  • Encourage self-harm.
  • Coordinate harmful actions.

13.5 Weapons and Dangerous Materials

The promotion, sale, trafficking, or unlawful distribution of weapons, explosives, dangerous substances, or other regulated items is prohibited except where expressly permitted by applicable laws and Platform policies.

13.6 Emergency Situations

Where BeTogether reasonably believes that a credible threat to safety exists, the Platform may:

  • Restrict User access.
  • Suspend accounts.
  • Preserve evidence.
  • Notify law enforcement authorities.
  • Cooperate with emergency investigations.

13.7 Immediate Enforcement

Violations involving threats, violence, terrorism, serious harassment, or credible safety risks may result in immediate account suspension or permanent termination without prior warning.

Protection of Community safety shall take precedence over continued Platform access.

Article 14 – User Profiles

14.1 Purpose of User Profiles

User Profiles serve as the primary means through which members of the BeTogether Community identify themselves, establish trust, showcase services or interests, and engage with other Users.

Profiles should accurately represent the individual or organization behind the Account.

14.2 Accuracy of Profile Information

Users must ensure that all profile information is:

  • Accurate
  • Current
  • Truthful
  • Complete

  • Identity
  • Qualifications
  • Skills
  • Certifications
  • Professional experience
  • Geographic location
  • Availability

Misleading profile information may result in profile restrictions or account suspension.

14.3 Profile Photos

Profile images must accurately represent the User.

The following are prohibited:

  • Images of other individuals without authorization.
  • Misleading artificial identities.
  • Offensive content.
  • Explicit sexual imagery.
  • Violent content.
  • Hate symbols.
  • Illegal content.

BeTogether reserves the right to remove profile images that violate Community Standards.

14.4 Professional Credentials

Users claiming professional qualifications must ensure that such claims are accurate and verifiable.

Examples include:

  • Legal qualifications.
  • Medical credentials.
  • Technical certifications.
  • Educational degrees.
  • Industry licenses.

False claims regarding professional qualifications constitute a serious violation.

14.5 Business Profiles

Organizations using BeTogether must provide accurate business information, including:

  • Legal business name.
  • Contact details.
  • Service descriptions.
  • Applicable licenses.

Business profiles may be subject to enhanced verification requirements.

14.6 Profile Visibility

Users are responsible for determining what information they choose to make publicly available.

Users should avoid publishing sensitive personal information that could create privacy or security risks.

15.1 Purpose of Communications

Communication tools are provided to facilitate legitimate interactions related to:

  • Services
  • Activities
  • Events
  • Community participation
  • Collaboration opportunities

Users must use communication tools responsibly and respectfully.

15.2 Respectful Communication Standards

All communications must remain:

  • Civil
  • Respectful
  • Relevant
  • Non-abusive

15.3 Spam and Unsolicited Communications

Users may not:

  • Send mass unsolicited messages.
  • Distribute spam.
  • Conduct unauthorized advertising campaigns.
  • Send repetitive promotional content.
  • Use automated messaging systems without authorization.

15.4 Fraudulent Communications

The following are prohibited:

  • Phishing attempts.
  • Impersonation.
  • Deceptive solicitations.
  • Fake investment opportunities.
  • Requests for sensitive information under false pretenses.

15.5 External Communications

Users who choose to communicate outside the Platform do so at their own risk.

BeTogether cannot monitor or control communications that occur through third-party services.

15.6 Record Preservation

BeTogether may retain communication records where necessary for:

  • Security investigations.
  • Legal compliance.
  • Fraud prevention.
  • Enforcement actions.

Retention practices are governed by applicable privacy laws and Platform policies.

Article 16 – Events and Meetups

16.1 Purpose of Events

BeTogether allows Users to organize, promote, and participate in events, meetups, experiences, activities, and social gatherings.

Events may be conducted online or in person.

16.2 Organizer Responsibilities

Event organizers are solely responsible for:

  • Event planning.
  • Participant management.
  • Venue compliance.
  • Legal compliance.
  • Safety considerations.
  • Insurance requirements where applicable.

BeTogether is not the organizer of User-created events.

16.3 Participant Responsibilities

Participants are responsible for:

  • Evaluating event suitability.
  • Assessing personal safety.
  • Following applicable laws.
  • Respecting organizers and attendees.

Participation in any event is voluntary and undertaken at the User's own discretion.

16.4 Safety Recommendations

Users are encouraged to:

  • Meet in public locations.
  • Inform trusted contacts of their plans.
  • Verify event details.
  • Exercise caution when attending unfamiliar activities.

16.5 Prohibited Events

Events involving the following are prohibited:

  • Illegal activities.
  • Violence.
  • Dangerous conduct.
  • Discrimination.
  • Hate groups.
  • Criminal enterprises.
  • Exploitation.

Such events may be removed without notice.

16.6 Event Cancellation

Organizers remain responsible for notifying participants regarding cancellations, postponements, modifications, or safety concerns.

BeTogether bears no responsibility for losses arising from event cancellations.

16.7 Liability for Offline Conduct

Users acknowledge that BeTogether does not supervise or control offline interactions.

Responsibility for conduct during events rests solely with participating Users.

BeTogether provides a platform through which Users may offer, request, discover, and exchange services.

BeTogether is not a party to agreements formed between Users.

17.2 Service Listings

  • Honest

  • Non-misleading

Users must clearly disclose:

  • Scope of services.

17.3 Prohibited Services

  • Promote fraud.

  • Licensing requirements.

17.5 User Agreements

  • Services

17.7 Marketplace Integrity

Users may not:

  • Manipulate listings.
  • Misrepresent services.

  • Create fake transactions.
  • Mislead potential clients.

Such conduct may result in immediate enforcement action.

Reviews and ratings help Users make informed decisions and promote accountability within the Community.

Users should provide honest, accurate, and fair feedback based on genuine experiences.

Reviews must reflect actual interactions.

Users may not:

  • Submit fake reviews.
  • Purchase reviews.

  • Coordinate rating manipulation.

18.3 Prohibited Review Content

Reviews may not contain:

  • Threats.
  • Harassment.
  • Hate speech.
  • Defamatory statements.
  • Private information.
  • Illegal content.

18.4 Retaliation Prohibition

Users may not retaliate against others for submitting reviews.

Examples include:

  • Harassment.
  • Threats.
  • Review manipulation.
  • Coordinated reporting campaigns.

18.5 Review Investigations

BeTogether may investigate suspicious review activity and may remove reviews where there is evidence of:

  • Fraud.
  • Manipulation.
  • Coordinated abuse.
  • Policy violations.

18.6 Review Removal

BeTogether reserves the right to remove reviews that violate Community Guidelines, legal obligations, or Platform policies.

Removal decisions are made at BeTogether's discretion.

18.7 No Guarantee of Accuracy

While BeTogether encourages honest feedback, Users acknowledge that reviews represent personal opinions and experiences.

BeTogether does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of any review or rating published on the Platform.

Article 19 – Safety During In-Person Meetings

19.1 General Safety Principle

BeTogether encourages meaningful real-world interactions while recognizing that offline meetings involve inherent risks.

Users are solely responsible for evaluating and managing their personal safety when participating in meetings, events, services, activities, collaborations, or transactions arranged through the Platform.

No verification process, profile information, review system, or communication history can completely eliminate risk.

19.2 Independent Risk Assessment

Before meeting another User, individuals should independently assess potential risks and determine whether participation is appropriate.

Users should consider:

  • The nature of the activity.
  • The location.
  • The identity of participants.
  • Available safety information.
  • Local conditions.
  • Personal circumstances.

19.3 Recommended Safety Measures

Users are strongly encouraged to:

  • Meet in public places whenever possible.
  • Inform trusted individuals of meeting details.
  • Maintain access to communication devices.
  • Arrange independent transportation.
  • Avoid isolated locations.
  • Verify identities when appropriate.
  • Trust personal instincts when concerns arise.

19.4 High-Risk Activities

Certain activities may involve elevated risks, including:

  • Physical activities.
  • Outdoor excursions.
  • Transportation services.
  • Home visits.
  • Late-night meetings.
  • Activities involving specialized equipment.

Participants remain responsible for evaluating whether they possess the necessary skills, qualifications, and preparation.

19.5 Personal Responsibility

Users acknowledge that they participate in offline interactions voluntarily and assume responsibility for decisions made before, during, and after such interactions.

BeTogether does not supervise, direct, control, or monitor offline activities.

19.6 Incident Reporting

Users should promptly report serious safety incidents that arise from Platform interactions.

Reports may assist BeTogether in identifying dangerous conduct and protecting the Community.

Article 20 – Fraud Prevention

20.1 Commitment to Trust

Fraud undermines confidence in the Community and threatens the integrity of the Platform.

BeTogether maintains a zero-tolerance approach toward fraudulent conduct.

20.2 Definition of Fraud

Fraud includes any intentional act designed to deceive, manipulate, exploit, or improperly obtain money, property, services, information, access, or benefits.

Fraud may occur through:

  • Misrepresentation.
  • False promises.
  • Deceptive transactions.
  • Identity misuse.
  • Financial schemes.

20.3 Prohibited Fraudulent Conduct

Examples include:

  • Fake service offerings.
  • False qualifications.
  • Nonexistent events.
  • Misleading advertisements.
  • Counterfeit documentation.
  • Fake business identities.
  • Payment fraud.

This list is illustrative rather than exhaustive.

20.4 Financial Misrepresentation

Users may not intentionally misrepresent:

  • Pricing.
  • Fees.
  • Costs.
  • Refund policies.
  • Service capabilities.
  • Business status.

Transparency is required in all commercial interactions.

20.5 Investigation of Fraud

BeTogether reserves the right to investigate suspected fraud and may request supporting documentation, identity verification, transaction records, or additional information.

Failure to cooperate may result in account restrictions.

20.6 Fraud Enforcement Measures

Confirmed fraud may result in:

  • Content removal.
  • Service restrictions.
  • Account suspension.
  • Permanent account termination.
  • Referral to law enforcement authorities.

Article 21 – Scams and Deceptive Practices

21.1 Prohibition of Scams

Users may not engage in any scheme intended to deceive, manipulate, exploit, or improperly influence other Users.

21.2 Common Scam Types

Prohibited activities include:

  • Advance-fee scams.
  • Fake investment opportunities.
  • Lottery scams.
  • Employment scams.
  • Romance scams.
  • Charity scams.
  • Cryptocurrency fraud.
  • Impersonation schemes.
