Article 1 – Company Information

1.1 Platform Operator

For administrative, technical, operational, regulatory, and publishing purposes, the BeTogether Platform may be operated and managed through:

TITANUS S.R.L.S.

Country of Incorporation: Italy

Legal Contact: legal@betogether.com

Website: betogetherapp.com

The use of TITANUS S.R.L.S. as an operating entity does not alter the ownership structure of the BeTogether project, which remains governed by the provisions set forth in this Legal Information document.

BeTogether is an independent digital platform conceived, designed, developed, and owned by its co-founders:

The BeTogether project, including its concept, business model, brand identity, platform architecture, strategic vision, software assets, databases, documentation, and all associated intellectual property rights, belongs exclusively to the co-founders unless otherwise established through a specific written agreement.

All rights relating to the BeTogether project are reserved.

1.3 Operational Use of TITANUS S.R.L.S.

The co-founders may utilize TITANUS S.R.L.S. solely as an operational and technical vehicle for purposes including, but not limited to:

  • Publication of the BeTogether mobile application on Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and other distribution channels;
  • Management of developer accounts;
  • Management of technical infrastructure;
  • Hosting and cloud services administration;
  • Operational support activities;
  • Administrative compliance;
  • Regulatory compliance;
  • Payment processing integrations;
  • Third-party service integrations.

The use of TITANUS S.R.L.S. shall not be interpreted as a transfer, assignment, sale, licensing, or conveyance of ownership rights relating to BeTogether.

1.4 Retention of Ownership Rights

Unless expressly agreed otherwise in a written instrument signed by all co-founders, TITANUS S.R.L.S. does not acquire any ownership interest in:

  • The BeTogether trademark;
  • The BeTogether brand;
  • The BeTogether platform;
  • The source code;
  • Databases;
  • User experience designs;
  • Business model;
  • Strategic assets;
  • Intellectual property;
  • Future developments of the project.

All ownership rights, economic rights, intellectual property rights, governance rights, and strategic control remain vested exclusively in the co-founders.

1.5 Founder Ownership

The BeTogether project is jointly owned by:

  • Marco Tancini

Each co-founder retains ownership rights according to the agreements established between the founders.

No provision contained within this document shall be interpreted as modifying, transferring, reducing, or extinguishing the rights of either co-founder.

1.6 No Automatic Transfer of Rights

The publication, operation, administration, promotion, commercialization, maintenance, or technical management of BeTogether through TITANUS S.R.L.S. shall not create any automatic transfer of:

  • Intellectual property rights;

2.1 Platform Description

BeTogether is a global digital platform designed to facilitate meaningful human connections through:

  • Services;
  • Activities;
  • Experiences;

  • Community engagement;
