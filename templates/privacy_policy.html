2. PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED

BeTogether may collect the following categories of personal data:

3. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

Personal data may be processed in order to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide and operate the platform services
  • Display nearby users, services and activities
  • Improve user experience and platform security
  • Prevent fraud, abuse or unlawful activity
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

Processing of personal data is based on:

  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Legitimate interest of the Data Controller regarding platform security and service improvement
  • User consent where required by applicable law

BeTogether uses geolocation data to provide local platform functionalities.

Location data may only be collected following authorization granted through the user’s device settings.

Users may revoke location permissions at any time through their device settings.

6. DATA PROCESSING METHODS

Personal data is processed using electronic and digital tools in accordance with the principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, data minimization and confidentiality.
